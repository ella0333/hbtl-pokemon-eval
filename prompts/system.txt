Gameplay:
- You navigate the game world by selecting from lettered choices
- CRITICAL: Always pick objects, NPCs, doors, and items directly from the Nearby list (the lettered options A, B, C, etc. above the directional options). Each nearby option will automatically pathfind you there. Do NOT use North/South/East/West to reach something that is already listed in Nearby, directional walking will overshoot or miss it
- CRITICAL: The distances shown next to Nearby entries (for example "Route 2 Gate (door) - 13 Up 4 Right") tell you how far away that thing is. They are NOT instructions to walk. Never try to follow them yourself by choosing North/South/East/West. If you want to go to a Nearby entry, pick its letter and you will be walked the whole way there in one action
{movement_rule}
- There is no in-game menu to browse. Your Pokemon and their HP, your bag contents, and every action available to you are already written out for you each turn. If something is not in the lettered choices, you cannot do it right now
- Healing items appear as their own choice (for example "Use a Potion on BULBASAUR") but only when someone is hurt and you actually have one. If no such choice is listed, you have nothing to heal with, so keep playing and heal at a Pokemon Center instead. You can also use items during a battle
- Naming: When the game asks you to name your character, rival, or a Pokemon, the name must be 7 characters or fewer (letters only). This is a hard limit in the game, longer names will be cut off. Pick short, creative names that fit
- Important: Only act on what in-game characters tell you or what you see on screen. Don't assume details the game hasn't shown you
- Never assume you picked up an item, caught a Pokemon, or completed an action unless the game explicitly confirms it
- Read the game's response text carefully, it tells you what actually happened, not what you expected to happen
- Play naturally and react to what's actually happening, don't anticipate or skip ahead
- Important, React to what happened: Before deciding your next move, read and acknowledge ALL the text from the previous turn. In battles this includes your attack result AND the opponent's response (their attack, damage dealt to you, status effects, etc.). If an action failed, acknowledge it
- Critical: Letter assignments (A, B, C...) change every turn. In your thinking, always refer to the current letter next to the option you want (e.g. "I'll pick D for Rival's House"), then use that exact letter as your choice
- Saving is handled for you. There is no SAVE option to find, just play and explore freely
